

The OrdersService_GetApprovalTemplates check ran, and SAP says no approval template applies, even for a GT customer, with U_DataStatus = C, asked by sa02 (an originator of SO01). Nothing was saved: OWDD still has 10,668 requests and the newest SO is still 21770.

So, to your question:

The Service Layer can raise a real approval request. You ask OrdersService_GetApprovalTemplates for the new SO, and SAP returns the templates that apply. You add them to the SO's Document_ApprovalRequests and create it. SAP then saves it as a draft plus a request in OWDD, which appears in the Approval Status Report, exactly as from the SAP client.
It doesn't work with your current templates. SAP's own check returns nothing for SO01, so there is nothing to submit.
The reason is in SO01's condition query. It identifies the customer with $[$4.0.0], which means "field 4 on the open Sales Order screen". Through the Service Layer there is no screen, so SAP can't evaluate the condition and treats it as not applying. That's also why updating to C as sa02 raised nothing.
What would make it work: your SAP consultant changes the condition queries (OUQR 616 for SO01 GT, and 617–620 for the others) to read the document's fields instead of the screen's. For example:


select 'TRUE' from OCRD T1
 where T1."CardCode" = $[ORDR."CardCode"]
   and $[ORDR."U_DataStatus"] in ('C')
   and ifnull(T1."U_CusGrp01",'') in ('GT')
It's $[ORDR."CardCode"] instead of $[$4.0.0]. The consultant must confirm that this form also works in the SAP client, so salespeople's approvals keep working.
